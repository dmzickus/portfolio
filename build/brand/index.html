The name

Every theater has an origin story. This one starts with a lizard.

Admit one · the originalSpottyLizard · namesake Spotty Connection

Spotty Connection is named after Spotty, the lizard we had. When we needed a name for our Wi-Fi network, I came up with "Spotty Connection," and it stuck.

Since then it has become the name for everything I run: the movies, the music, the photos and the rest of the homelab. The dead bulbs in the logo keep the joke going.

01

The mark

A Wi-Fi signal with burnt-out marquee bulbs sitting in its gaps. The connection is spotty because a few lights are dead.

Primary markSVG
  • The duds are fixedOne dead bulb in the middle arc and two in the outer arc. Never light them, move them, or add more. That pattern is the logo.
  • Teal inside, amber outsideThe inner arcs are signal teal. The outer arc and the access-point dot are concession amber.
  • Small sizes use the simple markAt 48 px and below, swap to the favicon version: the same arcs with plain gaps and no bulbs.
  • Don'tRecolor it, rotate it, add drop shadows, outline the wordmark, stretch a lockup, or put it on a photo without a dark plate.

07

Motion

Warm and analog: eased fades and flickers, no slides or bounces. The dot lights first, the arcs power on ring by ring, the dud bulbs sputter out, then the name comes up. Every animation respects reduced-motion settings.

Two modes

One brand, two lighting cues. House lights is the calm, professional version. Showtime is the full marquee. Both use the same mark, the same teal and amber, and the same dead bulbs.

House lights

Site · resume · LinkedIn · GitHub

  • Navy background, cool off-white text
  • Space Grotesk headings, mono labels
  • The mark and its power-on animation are the only marquee touches

Showtime

Self-hosted services · preshows · dashboards · stickers

  • Marquee black, projector cream, amber links
  • Bungee and Bebas Neue, chasing bulbs, poster cards
  • Statuses become Now showing, Coming soon, Intermission, Final curtain
Shared in both
  • The hybrid mark
  • Teal = live / links
