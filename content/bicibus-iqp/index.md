The IQP is WPI's required project on how technology and society interact. Ours is with **LlactaLAB** in Cuenca, Ecuador, studying the city's **Bicibus**: groups of kids riding to school together along a set route with adult escorts.

My part of the background research looks at bike buses around the world. I'm looking at which pieces make a program last (routes, volunteers, schools, city support) and which of those carry over to Latin American cities. We're also planning interviews with potential funders and partners to understand how the program could be paid for.

Personally, I'm hoping to come back with much better spoken Spanish.
