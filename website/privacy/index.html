Privacy Policy

How my personal tools and the "rclone" Google OAuth app handle data.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the personal Google OAuth app "rclone" registered by Dylan Zickus and any small tools on dylanzickus.com. These apps are for my own personal and household use only. They are not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

The rclone app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can only see and manage files that it created itself in my own Google Drive. It cannot read any other files in Google Drive.

How data is used

  • The app only uploads encrypted backups of my personal home-server data to my own Google Drive, and reads them back when restoring.
  • Backups are encrypted before they leave my server. Google cannot read their contents.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on my own server and are never shared.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
